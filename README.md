# CRM Lead & Opportunity Dashboard

## 📊 Project Overview

This project presents an interactive **CRM Lead & Opportunity Dashboard** designed to analyse the complete sales funnel from Lead Creation → Opportunity → Won/Lost.

The dashboard analyses **5,000 CRM leads created between January and September 2026** and provides insights into sales conversion, pipeline value, campaign performance, follow-up health, regional and city-wise performance, pipeline ageing, deal-size conversion and lost leads.

The objective of the project is to transform CRM data into actionable business insights that can support sales management, pipeline monitoring and process improvement.

---

## 🎯 Business Objectives

The dashboard was developed to help management and sales teams:

* Monitor the Lead → Opportunity → Won/Lost funnel
* Track overall conversion and loss rates
* Analyse open pipeline and pipeline value
* Measure campaign and lead-source effectiveness
* Identify overdue and missing follow-ups
* Monitor inactive and ageing leads
* Compare regional and city-wise performance
* Analyse conversion by deal size
* Identify lost-lead patterns
* Monitor sales representative performance
* Identify areas requiring management attention

## 📈 Dashboard Analysis

### 1. Lead → Opportunity → Won Funnel

The funnel tracks the movement of leads through different CRM stages:

**New → Qualified → Proposal → Negotiation → Won / Lost**

Key observations include:

* 5,000 leads were created during the analysis period.
* 4,151 leads progressed beyond the New stage.
* 2,772 leads remained in the active pipeline.
* 638 leads resulted in Sales Orders.
* 741 leads were marked as Lost.
* 849 leads remained in the New stage.

The dashboard also calculates a probability-weighted forecast based on opportunity stage.

### 3. Activity & Follow-up Health

The dashboard monitors CRM activity and follow-up discipline.

Key metrics include:

* Future follow-ups
* Overdue follow-ups
* Leads without a follow-up date
* Days since last activity
* Leads with 30+ days inactivity
* Leads with 60+ days inactivity
* Leads with 90+ days inactivity

A major focus of the analysis is identifying stale pipeline and improving follow-up discipline.

---

### 4. Region-wise Analysis

Lead performance is analysed across:

* West
* North
* South
* Central
* East

The dashboard compares:

* Lead volume
* Pipeline value
* Won %
* Lost %
* Open pipeline

This helps management identify regional differences in sales conversion and losses.

---

### 5. City-wise Analysis

The dashboard provides city-level performance analysis using:

* Lead count
* Won %
* Lost %
* Opportunity value

The analysis covers 25 cities including Mumbai, Pune, Surat, Nashik, Ahmedabad, Delhi, Noida, Bengaluru, Chennai and others.

---

### 6. Pipeline Ageing

Open opportunities are grouped into ageing buckets:

* 0–30 days
* 31–60 days
* 61–90 days
* 91–180 days
* 180+ days

This helps identify ageing opportunities and pipeline value that may require immediate attention.

---

### 7. Conversion by Deal Size

The dashboard analyses conversion according to opportunity value bands, including:

* < ₹3L
* ₹3–5L
* ₹5–10L
* ₹10–15L
* ₹15–25L

This provides visibility into how deal size relates to Won and Lost rates.

---

### 8. Lost Lead Analysis

Lost leads are analysed by:

* Region
* Campaign
* Deal size
* Cycle time

The source data does not contain a dedicated **Loss Reason** field. Therefore, the dashboard identifies patterns using available fields and recommends introducing a mandatory Loss Reason field in the CRM.

Possible future Loss Reason categories could include:

* Price
* Timing
* Competitor
* No Budget
* Unresponsive
* Requirement Changed

---

### 9. Sales Team Analysis

The dashboard also provides sales-representative level analysis including:

* Leads generated
* Won rate
* Won value
* Pipeline contribution

This allows management to distinguish between representatives who generate higher conversion rates and those who generate higher absolute sales value.

---

## 🔍 Key Business Insights

Some important findings from the analysis include:

* A significant portion of open leads have not had recent activity.
* A large number of open leads have overdue follow-ups.
* The open pipeline contains a substantial amount of ageing opportunities.
* Regional and city-level conversion rates vary considerably.
* Campaign volume does not necessarily translate into higher conversion.
* Larger-value opportunities show different Won/Lost behaviour compared with smaller opportunities.
* The absence of a Loss Reason field limits root-cause analysis of Lost leads.
* Repeat customers represent a potential segment for separate tracking and follow-up.

---

## 💡 Recommended Business Actions

Based on the dashboard analysis:

1. Make **Next Follow-up Date** mandatory during relevant CRM stage changes.
2. Create alerts for leads without recent activity.
3. Monitor overdue follow-ups through periodic sales reports.
4. Review opportunities that remain open for extended periods.
5. Introduce a mandatory **Loss Reason** field.
6. Monitor campaign quality in addition to campaign volume.
7. Analyse city and region performance regularly.
8. Track deal-size conversion separately.
9. Monitor repeat customers as a separate segment.
10. Use weighted forecast values for pipeline planning.
